How it works

  • Every gameweek, pick one Premier League team to win.
  • They win, you go through. A draw puts you out, same as a defeat.
  • Once you've used a team, you can't use them again until everyone's burnt through all twenty.
  • Picks lock at the first kick-off of the gameweek. No pick means you're out, so don't leave it to Saturday morning.
  • If your team's match is postponed, you survive the round and get that team back.
  • If everyone left goes out in the same gameweek, nobody goes out — the round is void and you all carry on.
  • Everyone's picks stay hidden until the deadline passes. Then the board shows the lot.
  • One life. Last one standing takes the pot.
